About Candylicious

Your one-stop destination for global confectionery and novelty brands.

Our Story

Candylicious is the first of its kind in South Africa, combining global confectionery, novelty brands and merchandise from the US, the UK and Europe, all within one world-class concept store.

The idea of Candylicious was conceptualised in 2012 and developed into a store-within-a-store concept. Our ever-changing range means there is always something new and exciting to discover.

The principles that guide the Candylicious experience.

To offer trusted brands, exciting variety and a fun shopping experience for every customer.
