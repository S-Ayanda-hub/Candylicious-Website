    • About Us

    Candylicious is the first of its kind in South Africa, combining global confectionery, novelty brands and merchandise from the US, the UK and Europe, all within one world-class concept store. The idea of Candylicious was conceptualised in 2012 and developed into a “store within a store” concept. We combined the top international brands such as M&M, Hersheys, Reeses, Jelly Belly, Cadbury, Nestle and many more all within one store giving our customers a world of treats to choose from. These brands are known and trusted to be of the highest quality, guaranteeing our customers 100% satisfaction and enjoyment with every bite. Candylicious South Africa has become known for its ever changing ranges of products, always something new and exciting.


    Our Mission

    “Treat Yourself” by making world-class international confectionery and novelties accessible, exciting and affordable to every South African shopper.


    Our Vision

    “To be South Africas leading destination for global candy and novelty brands, both online and in-store.”
