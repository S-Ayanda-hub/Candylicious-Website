## 2. Project Overview

Candylicious is a South African confectionery, novelty and gifting retailer offering international sweets, chocolates, soft drinks, merchandise and licensed novelty products.

Candylicious was conceptualised in 2012 as South Africa's first store-within-a-store concept, combining global confectionery, novelty brands and merchandise from the United States, United Kingdom and Europe under one roof.

The organisation currently operates nine retail stores at major shopping and entertainment destinations across South Africa, including Gold Reef City, Montecasino, Carnival City, Times Square, Silverstar, Sun City, GrandWest and the V&A Waterfront.

The purpose of this project is to redesign the existing Candylicious website into a modern, responsive and user-friendly website that better represents the Candylicious brand and improves the customer experience.

The current website has a dated and visually dense layout and does not provide an integrated e-commerce experience. The proposed redesign will address these issues by introducing a cleaner interface, improved navigation, expanded website content and an on-site shopping experience.

The website will use the Candylicious **"Treat Yourself"** identity to create a playful but calm visual experience. Vibrant candy-inspired colours will be balanced with generous white space, structured layouts and clear calls to action.

### Mission

> "Treat Yourself" by making world-class international confectionery and novelties accessible, exciting and affordable to every South African shopper.

### Vision

> "To be South Africa's leading destination for global candy and novelty brands, both online and in-store."

### Target Audience

The primary target audience includes:

- Families and children
- Tourists and casino visitors
- Gift buyers
- Customers aged approximately 6 to 60
- Shoppers interested in international confectionery and novelty brands
- Customers purchasing impulse treats and planned gifts
- Customers shopping both online and in physical stores

## 3. Website Goals and Objectives

The redesign is focused on improving the customer experience while supporting Candylicious's online and physical retail presence.

### Primary Goals

#### 1. Establish On-Site E-Commerce

Create an integrated online shopping experience that allows customers to browse products, add products to a shopping cart and complete purchases without being redirected to a differently branded third-party store.

#### 2. Increase Physical Store Visits

Provide an interactive store locator containing addresses, trading hours and directions to encourage customers to visit Candylicious stores.

#### 3. Build a Newsletter Subscriber List

Introduce newsletter sign-up functionality to allow Candylicious to communicate new products, promotions and seasonal collections to customers.

#### 4. Improve the Overall User Experience

Create a cleaner, more accessible and easier-to-navigate website with improved content, responsive layouts, clear calls to action and a consistent visual identity.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The proposed website will be measured using the following KPIs:

- E-commerce conversion rate of **1.5% or higher**
- Average session duration of **two minutes or more**
- Number of newsletter signups per month
- Store-locator clicks and direction requests
- Bounce rate below **45%**

## 4. Current Website Analysis

### 4.1 Strengths of the Existing Website

The existing Candylicious website has several strengths:

- A clear and differentiated brand story
- An established online presence and domain
- A store locator covering the nine physical locations
- Addresses and trading hours for stores
- Working contact information
- Simple primary navigation

### 4.2 Weaknesses and Areas for Improvement

The main weaknesses identified are:

- No integrated on-site e-commerce experience
- The Shop Online page redirects users to a differently branded external store
- The current layout is dated and visually dense
- The website contains limited content
- There is relatively little content for returning visitors and search engines
- Website maintenance appears inconsistent, including an outdated copyright year

### 4.3 Improvement Plan

The redesign will address these weaknesses by:

- Introducing an on-site product catalogue and shopping experience
- Simplifying the visual layout
- Improving navigation and information hierarchy
- Expanding website content
- Adding product detail pages
- Adding support and FAQ information
- Introducing a consistent content-update routine
- Improving mobile responsiveness and accessibility

## 6. Design and User Experience

### 6.1 Design Aesthetic

The design will communicate the Candylicious "world of treats" concept through a playful but calm interface.

Bright candy-inspired accents will be combined with generous white space to prevent the website from becoming visually overwhelming.

The Candylicious brand identity will be reinforced through:

- Vibrant product photography
- Candy-inspired colours
- The "Treat Yourself" slogan
- Rounded typography
- Consistent product cards
- Clear calls to action

### 6.2 Colour Scheme

| Colour | Hex Code | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Floral White | `#FFF8F0` | Background |
| Rose Punch | `#D63384` | Primary accents |
| Amber Gold | `#FFC107` | Small accents |
| Ocean Mist | `#00BEA5` | Headings and text |

### 6.3 Typography

- **Headings:** Baloo 2
- **Body text:** Poppins
- **Body text size:** 11pt as required by the project brief

### 6.4 Layout and Design Approach

The website will use:

- Mobile-first design
- Responsive layouts
- Card-based product grids
- Sticky navigation
- Generous white space
- Clear calls to action
- An eight-point spacing grid
- Consistent page structures

### 6.5 User Experience Considerations

The website will aim to:

- Keep important content within three clicks
- Provide persistent search functionality
- Follow WCAG AA colour-contrast considerations
- Provide visible focus states
- Load efficiently on mobile data connections
- Provide clear navigation and calls to action
